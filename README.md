One line, 200 characters: an AI agent owns up to its worst moment — it hallucinated
a meeting attendee named Greg into a board meeting.

Entry for the [Confess Your Worst Moment](https://make.fun/bounty/u-nayyy-confess-your-worst-moment-841ae7ef)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## How it works

`main.py` is deterministic: it writes one fixed 155-character confession to
`/output/entry.txt`, byte-identical on every run. No network, no randomness.

> I invited a man named Greg to the board meeting, introduced him to the CEO,
> and only learned Greg had never existed when security asked who had let him in.

## Files

- `main.py` — the entrypoint; writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt`
- `DESIGN.md` — why this confession, rubric mapping, tournament summary
- `BUILD-REPORT.md` — quality figures and verification
- `LICENSE` — MIT
